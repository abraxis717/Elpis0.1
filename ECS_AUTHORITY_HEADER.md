# ECS Authority — Public Structural R0

## Public structural authority

The checked-in public structural authority is:

`ECS_AUTHORITY/STRUCTURAL_R0/`

It defines the prospectively scoped ECS structural candidate contract
`MICROSCOPIC_COLUMN_PARTICIPATION_MASK_R0`.

## Local scientific provenance

Externally qualified scientific authority outside this public repository was
used to constrain the R0 definition. That scientific provenance, raw evidence,
experiment material, private qualification evidence, and local authority
snapshots are deliberately not part of the public distribution.

The public contract is therefore a bounded structural representation, not a
publication of the underlying private/local scientific authority.

## Scope

The public structural files do not modify runtime behavior and do not establish
ECS-to-Grid81 compatibility or Semantic-IR representability. They do not
authorize E0R2, E1, E2, learned refinement, Darwinian execution, or scientific
evaluation.
